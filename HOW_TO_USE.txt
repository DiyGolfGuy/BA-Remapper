BA REMAPPER 5.1.3 - HOW TO USE
BA Custom Products LLC | bacustomproducts.com

1. INSTALL OR UPDATE

Download BARemapper_Setup_v5.1.3.zip from the BA website or:
https://github.com/DiyGolfGuy/BA-Remapper/releases/latest

Extract the ZIP. If Remapper is running, right-click its green BA tray
icon and choose Exit. Run the Setup EXE and follow the installer.
Installing over your old version keeps profiles and settings.

Open BA Remapper from the Start menu. This guide is also available there
as BA Remapper How To. The portable ZIP includes the same guide; extract
all its files into a permanent folder and run BARemapper.exe.

Requires 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 and GSPro. Smart Clicks and Scramble
Auto-Pick use built-in Windows OCR. They do not upload screen images.

2. USE THE PRINTED CONTROL-BOX FUNCTIONS

Select Basic Secondary. Turn Button Mapping ON.
Tap a button for its white printed function.
Hold Reset Aim (A), then press another button for its yellow function.
Reset Aim is the default FN button. Keep it held for repeated actions.

FN + Club Up or Club Down: Camera Clip / Clear View (B)
FN + Tee Left:             OB Rehit
FN + Aim Up:               Move Forward
FN + Aim Down:             Move Back
FN + Aim Left:             Next Option
FN + Aim Right:            Drop Ball / Rehit

The relief menu must be visible for its Smart Click actions. The first
successful use reads and learns the menu; later presses verify smaller
areas at the learned spots for faster response.

Ctrl+F12 toggles Button Mapping. Turn mapping OFF to type normally.
Closing the main window keeps the app running in the tray; Exit quits.

3. TURN ON SCRAMBLE AUTO-PICK AND THE CARD HIGHLIGHT

Open BA Remapper and check Auto-Pick Scramble Shot (all profiles).
Choose a delay: 5, 10, 15 or 20 seconds. Start with 15 seconds so the
players have time to see the choice and choose differently if desired.
Check Show chosen card highlight and countdown.
Return to GSPro and play a scramble with two, three or four cards.

When the shot-selection cards appear, Remapper reads them repeatedly.
A bright green outline marks the card currently leading the automatic
choice. A panel beneath the cards shows:

- CARD number: the card's left-to-right position, 1 through 4.
- Distance: feet/inches or yards, followed by lie and shot number.
- Reason: Fewest strokes, Green preferred, or Shortest distance.
- Countdown: seconds until the automatic choice is due.

Example: CARD 2 | 12s, then 87 yd | FAIRWAY | Shot 2 means card 2 is
currently the planned pick, at 87 yards, with 12 seconds remaining.
The choice can update as additional reads vote.

4. UNDERSTAND THE CHOICE AND OVERRIDE IT

The rules run in this order:
1) Fewest strokes first. A 2nd-shot ball beats a closer 3rd-shot ball.
2) Among those balls, GREEN is preferred.
3) Shortest distance decides among the remaining eligible balls.

Other lies are not ranked: it does not prefer fairway over rough or
avoid trees. Choose manually if you want a different ball for strategy.

A single mark ' (or a curved/prime equivalent) means feet. The double
mark " means inches. 14' 7" means fourteen feet, seven inches. One yard
is three feet. An unmarked number is feet on GREEN and yards elsewhere.
FRINGE is not GREEN; green status comes from the card's lie row.

You can click any GSPro USE button before the automatic choice. The
outline and panel let clicks through and do not take keyboard focus.
When the card screen closes, Remapper stops that selection cycle. It
also checks that the USE buttons are still present before sending the
choice at zero. Do not wait for a Pick Now or Skip button: this version
uses GSPro's existing USE buttons and the automatic countdown.

Reading Cards means there is no current readable preview. Checking at
zero means it is confirming/re-reading. If it cannot decide, choose a
card yourself. Turning off the highlight checkbox hides the visuals;
turn off Auto-Pick Scramble Shot to disable automatic choices entirely.

Auto-Pick is independent of Button Mapping and works across profiles.
Turning Button Mapping OFF does not turn Scramble Auto-Pick OFF.

5. SCREENS, CUSTOM PROFILES AND STARTUP

Keep GSPro active to see the highlight on its game display. It follows
the detected game window on multi-screen desktops. Moving or resizing
GSPro hides an old outline until a fresh read supplies its new position.
A minimized/inactive game, missing cards or stale positions hide it too.
Taught Screen Clicks store desktop positions; re-teach them after moving
the target. They do not automatically follow a moved game window.

To customize: choose Customize a Copy, name it, open Button Builder,
select a button, choose Hotkey, Smart Click, Taught Screen Click or None,
review the preview and Save. Cancel leaves the old action in place.
Basic Secondary stays locked to the printed yellow functions.

For Windows startup, choose Use Current Profile at Windows Startup and
check Start with Windows. The startup profile is separate from the one
you are currently editing. Windows startup loads it with mapping ON.

6. IF SOMETHING LOOKS WRONG

Wrong learned Smart Click: release FN and choose Reset Smart Click
Spots. The next use reads and learns again. Profiles, preferences and
Taught Screen Click positions stay. Factory Reset is a separate action.

No highlight: check both scramble checkboxes, make GSPro active and
ensure at least two USE cards are visible. The panel can briefly hide
while it re-reads uncertain card positions or during an OCR scan.

Want normal typing: Button Mapping OFF (Ctrl+F12).
Want no automatic scramble choice: uncheck Auto-Pick Scramble Shot.
Camera Clip: use Basic Secondary, turn mapping ON, hold Reset Aim (A)
and press either club button; release FN afterward.

If it picks incorrectly or stands down, use Open Folder and keep
scramble_last_decision.txt and scramble_log.txt for troubleshooting.
OCR Test reads the screen for a diagnostic report without selecting a
scramble card. Send the report with the GSPro/card screenshot when
asking BA Custom Products for help.
